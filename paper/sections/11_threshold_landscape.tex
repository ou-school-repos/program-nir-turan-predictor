\section{Threshold Landscape Data}

Table~\ref{tab:landscape_full} extends the threshold landscape
(Table~\ref{tab:thresholds}) to confirm that the vertex count valley
at $n = 13$ is the minimum within the tested $T(x,y,z)$ family.

\begin{table}[H]

\smallskip

\noindent{\footnotesize All 134{,}391{,}973 graphs $T(x,1,z)$ with
$|V| = 1 + 2x + xz \le 10{,}000{,}000$ were tested exhaustively
against $E_n^{(2)}$ for odd $n \le 51$ (4\,min, 6 threads).
Each minimizer in this table is unique: a tie would require distinct $(x_1,z_1),(x_2,z_2)$
with $x_1(2{+}z_1)=x_2(2{+}z_2)$ and the same crossover threshold,
a Diophantine coincidence that does not occur in this range.}
